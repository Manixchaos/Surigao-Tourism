Language & Phrases in Surigao Del Sur

The people of Surigao del Sur are warm and welcoming. Learning a few local words and phrases will go a long way in connecting with the locals and making your visit even more memorable!

Languages Spoken

The primary language spoken in Surigao del Sur is Surigaonon, a regional language closely related to Cebuano and Waray. Cebuano (Bisaya) is also widely understood and spoken across most municipalities. Filipino and English are used in schools, government offices, and business establishments. Indigenous communities such as the Manobo and Mamanwa have their own distinct languages and dialects as well.

BASIC GREETINGS!

Start your trip by saying hello the local way!

Everyday Greetings

Maayong buntag! — Good morning!
Maayong hapon! — Good afternoon!
Maayong gabii! — Good evening!
Kumusta ka? — How are you?
Maayo man, salamat! — I'm fine, thank you!
Unsa imong ngalan? — What is your name?
Ako si ___. — My name is ___.
