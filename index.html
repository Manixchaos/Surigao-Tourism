About Enchanted River

The Enchanted River, also called Hinatuan Sacred River, inspires many with its unusual sapphire and jade colors and unexplored depth. Locals believe "Enkantos" dwell within its waters.

How Deep is it?

It is roughly 24m (80 feet) deep. An underground cave system, first explored in 1999, continues to be a source of wonder, with openings discovered as deep as 30 meters.
