Beaches and Islands in Surigao Del Sur

This are the beaches and islands that you might want to consider when visiting!

Cagwait White Beach

Visitors can enjoy a variety of activities, from swimming and sunbathing to beach volleyball and picnicking. The local community is welcoming, and the area maintains a charming, laid-back atmosphere, preserving its natural allure. Come and discover the unspoiled beauty of Cagwait White Beach!

Visit this Beach Island as Well!

This is also a well known and most visited island in Surigao Del Sur!

Hagonoy Island Beach

Hagonoy Island is a picturesque, tiny white-sand islet in Surigao del Sur, Philippines, famous as part of the Britania Group of Islands in San Agustin. It is known for its powdery sand, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and coconut trees, usually visited via boat tours for swimming and sunbathing.
