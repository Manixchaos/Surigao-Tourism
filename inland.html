Inland Attractions Here in Surigao Del Sur!

Surigao Del Sur is home to breathtaking inland attractions! Beyond its beaches, discover lush caves, majestic waterfalls, and serene rivers waiting to be explored.

Hinayagan Cave

Located in Tagbina, Hinayagan Cave is one of the most fascinating cave systems in Surigao del Sur. The name "Hinayagan" means "lighted" in the local dialect, as sunlight penetrates deep into the cave, illuminating its stunning stalactites and stalagmites in a magical glow.

Bogac Cold Spring

Nestled near the famous Enchanted River in Hinatuan, Bogac Cold Spring is a naturally formed pool surrounded by towering palm trees and dense tropical forest. Its refreshingly cool and crystal-clear waters make it a perfect stop for a relaxing dip in nature.

Laswitan Falls and Lagoon

Found in Cortes, Laswitan is a unique natural wonder where powerful ocean waves crash over a 20-foot rock formation and cascade into a calm natural lagoon. The area features three distinct lagoons, each offering a different swimming experience framed by dramatic seawater sprays.
