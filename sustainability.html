Sustainability in Surigao Del Sur

Surigao del Sur is committed to responsible and sustainable tourism that protects its natural wonders for future generations. The province believes that loving the land means taking care of it — always.

Protecting the Enchanted River

Since 2017, swimming in the main pool of the Enchanted River has been restricted to preserve the health of its unique ecosystem. The river underwent a two-month closure for cleanup and rehabilitation before reopening with stricter environmental rules. Food and vehicles are now prohibited within the lagoon park area, and a designated swimming zone has been established to minimize human impact on the river's extraordinary natural environment.

Preserving marine life for the future!

Marine Protected Areas

The Lanuza Marine Park and Sanctuary covers over 111 hectares of protected waters and serves as a refuge for endangered species including the Philippine pawikan or sea turtle. Local government units across the province are also establishing marine-protected areas for diving and snorkeling to ensure that coral reefs and aquatic life remain healthy and thriving for generations of future visitors.

Empowering communities through tourism!

Community-Based Tourism

The Provincial Tourism Office actively partners with local communities, TESDA, and the Department of Tourism to ensure that the benefits of tourism reach the people living in and around tourist destinations. Programs like the Panuyamsuyam embroidery training help indigenous peoples generate sustainable income from their traditional crafts, keeping their culture alive while building local livelihoods.
