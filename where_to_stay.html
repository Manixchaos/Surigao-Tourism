Where to Stay in Surigao Del Sur

Whether you prefer budget-friendly inns or nature resort experiences, Surigao del Sur has accommodation options to suit every type of traveler. Tandag City offers the widest variety of hotels, while other municipalities have local resorts and guesthouses close to the attractions.

Tandag City — Main Hub

As the provincial capital and main gateway, Tandag City offers the most accommodation options in Surigao del Sur. You will find hotels, inns, and pension houses ranging from budget to mid-range prices. Staying in Tandag is ideal if you plan to explore multiple destinations across the province, as it sits centrally and has good transport connections to nearby municipalities.

Villa Azarcon Mountain Resort — Tago

Located in Barangay Cabangahan, Tago, Villa Azarcon Resort is a scenic mountain resort built around natural waterfalls and swimming pools. It is a perfect venue for family bonding, garden weddings, and birthday celebrations. The resort offers a refreshing escape into nature while providing essential guest amenities, making it one of the most beloved resorts in Surigao del Sur.

Local Resorts near Hinatuan & Bislig

Near the Enchanted River in Hinatuan and Tinuy-an Falls in Bislig, several locally managed resorts and guesthouses are available for overnight stays. These are ideal for travelers who want to experience the waterfalls and river during early morning or late afternoon when crowds are lighter. Booking in advance is recommended especially during peak season from November to May.
