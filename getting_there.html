Getting to Surigao Del Sur

Surigao del Sur is located on the northeastern coast of Mindanao facing the Pacific Ocean. While it may take a little effort to reach, every traveler who makes the journey agrees that the breathtaking sights are absolutely worth it.

By Air — Tandag Airport

The most convenient way to reach Surigao del Sur is by flying into Tandag Airport (TDG), the provincial capital's airport. Cebu Pacific operates regular flights connecting Tandag City to Cebu, making the province more accessible to travelers from the Visayas and other parts of the Philippines. From the airport, you can hire a tricycle or van to reach your destination within the province.

By Land — From Butuan City

Butuan City in Agusan del Norte serves as the main land gateway to Surigao del Sur. From Butuan, you can ride a van or bus going south toward Tandag City, which takes approximately 3 to 4 hours depending on road conditions. From Tandag, you can then travel further to municipalities like Hinatuan, Bislig, or San Agustin by van or habal-habal motorcycle.

Getting Around — Habal-Habal & Vans

Within Surigao del Sur, the most common modes of transport are habal-habal (motorcycle taxis) and passenger vans. Habal-habals are ideal for reaching remote attractions like waterfalls and springs that are off the main road. Vans ply the major routes between towns and can be hired for full-day tours. Always negotiate fares before boarding and confirm travel time with your driver.
