Provincial Tourism Office

The Surigao del Sur Provincial Tourism Office is located at the Provincial Capitol Compound in Tandag City. It is the primary office for tourism information, travel assistance, and coordination of tourism programs across the province. The office works closely with municipal tourism officers to help visitors plan safe and enjoyable experiences throughout Surigao del Sur.
