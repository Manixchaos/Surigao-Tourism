Arts & Crafts of Surigao Del Sur

The indigenous peoples of Surigao del Sur have a rich tradition of handcrafting beautiful works of art passed down through generations. From intricate beadwork to woven textiles, these crafts tell the story of a culture deeply rooted in nature and community.

Panuhugtuhug — Traditional Beadwork

The Panuhugtuhug is a traditional beadwork craft handmade by the indigenous Peoples (IP) communities of Surigao del Sur. These intricately designed bead accessories are worn during rituals, festivals, and special occasions. Each piece reflects the cultural identity of the tribe that created it, often featuring patterns that carry deep spiritual and ancestral meaning.

Panuyamsuyam — Traditional Embroidery

Panuyamsuyam is the art of traditional embroidery practiced by the Manobo tribe of Surigao del Sur. The Provincial Tourism Office, in partnership with TESDA, has been conducting training programs to preserve and revive this ancient craft in communities such as Sitio Ibuan in Lanuza. Manobo vest making is a key part of this tradition, producing beautifully ornamented garments worn during cultural celebrations.

Indigenous Weaving & Textiles

The indigenous communities of Surigao del Sur are known for their skillfully woven textiles using natural fibers and plant-based dyes. These handwoven fabrics are crafted into traditional clothing, bags, and decorative items. Visitors can find these products in local markets and cultural centers, offering a genuine piece of Surigaonon heritage to bring home as a souvenir.
