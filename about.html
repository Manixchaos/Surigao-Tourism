About Surigao Del Sur

Welcome to the land of wonders and pure bliss — proudly known as "Shangrila in the Pacific."

Location & Geography

Situated on the northeastern coast of Mindanao facing the Pacific Ocean, it stretches 300km in length. It is bounded by the Diwata Mountain Range and is a key part of the Caraga Region.
