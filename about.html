About Surigao Del Sur

Welcome to the land of wonders and pure bliss — Surigao del Sur, proudly known as "Shangrila in the Pacific" and home to some of the most breathtaking natural attractions in the Philippines.

Location & Geography

Surigao del Sur is one of five provinces in the Caraga Region, situated on the northeastern coast of Mindanao facing the Pacific Ocean. It stretches approximately 300 kilometers in length and 50 kilometers at its widest point. It is bounded by Surigao del Norte to the northwest, Davao Oriental to the southeast, the Pacific Ocean to the east, and the provinces of Agusan del Norte and Agusan del Sur to the west and southwest. The Diwata Mountain Range lines its northwestern boundaries.

Economy & Natural Resources

Surigao del Sur is rich in both natural beauty and natural resources. The province is one of the most significant sources of silver, chromite, and copper in the Philippines and across Asia. Agriculture also thrives here, with abundant tropical fruits, banana, and rice production. In recent years, tourism has become a growing driver of the local economy, supported by the province's slogan: "It's a Sur Thing."
